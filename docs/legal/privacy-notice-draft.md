# Privacy notice for members (draft)

> **Draft for legal review.** The customer's admin sends this to every member before capture
> starts. It matches Settings → Privacy in the product; keep the two in step.

**[Company] is piloting Master Mind**, a tool that learns which ways of working lead to good
results and shares them as practices your team approves. Here is what it does with information
about you.

## What it collects

- **From your AI agents** (if you connect them): a title and summary of each task, links to the
  work, and one-line notes of each step (which tool, which file and lines, which command). Never
  file contents or tool output.
- **From GitHub** (if connected): pull request titles, descriptions and metadata (files changed,
  reviews, CI results). Never code.
- **From HubSpot** (if connected): sequences, marketing emails and deals you own, with counts of
  replies, clicks and meetings, and deal stages. Never contact details, message contents or deal
  amounts.
- **From spreadsheets** an admin uploads: results of work, credited to you by your work email.
- **Your account:** name, work email, and which teams you're in.

Passwords, keys, emails, phone numbers and colleagues' names are removed from captured text
before anything is stored. Raw data is deleted after 30 days.

## How it's used

- To work out which practices lead to good results, **for the team as a whole**. Master Mind
  never shows success or failure per person, and team success rates appear only when at least
  five people contributed.
- To tell you and a teammate when your agents are changing the same thing at the same time.
- Your agent and a teammate's can exchange messages only if **you both approve** each time.
- Practices reach anyone's agents only after a person approves them. You're credited by name
  only if you choose to be.

## Your choices

- **Opt out** of capture at any time under Settings → Privacy: from then on, nothing about your
  work is collected from any source, and you don't appear as active to teammates.
- Mark an agent session as **private** so no one else sees it.
- Ask your admin to **remove you and erase** what was captured about you.

## Who can see it

Your teammates' agents can find past work when they search; they see your name only if you share
a team and your settings allow it. Master Mind staff access data only to run and support the
service. Data is stored in the United States, on Amazon Web Services, encrypted.

**Questions:** **[admin contact]** at [Company], or **[privacy contact]** at Master Mind.
